Cafés with play areas

Coffee for you, playtime for them.

COZY
VIBES
Le Brot

Both Le Brot locations in Neukölln have a small play area. At the Nansenstraße location, there's a little playhouse with removable parts, children's books and a box full of toys. Perfect for enjoying a croissant while your little one stays entertained.

Neukölln, Berlin
CUTE
PLAY
Kap Frida

A two-level play area with lots of books and building toys. The café is small, but the play area is usually quiet on weekdays. There's room to bring your stroller inside and park it safely. And the changing table is absolutely wonderful!

Halbinsel Stralau, Berlin
SAND
PLAY
Kiezkind Berlin

One of the coolest cafés for kids in Berlin! There's a large sandpit and toys scattered throughout the café. In summer, Bobby Cars are parked outside for little ones to ride. No entrance fee – you only pay for what you order.

Prenzlauer Berg, Berlin

Family-friendly restaurants

Good food, happy kids, less stress.

KIDS
MENU
Block House

This place really knows how to welcome families with young children! There's space to park your stroller next to the table, high chairs, a kids' menu, a changing table and toys brought over by the staff. Before you even order, your child gets crayons and a colouring sheet. After the meal, there's often another little surprise to take home, like a teddy bear or a puzzle.

Friedrichshain, Berlin
LOVELY
STAFF
Luisenbräu

A friend recommended this place, and she couldn't stop talking about it! The food is delicious, the portions are generous, and the staff is incredibly welcoming. ‘I thought it would be a tourist trap, but the quality surprised me. Our baby was asleep when we arrived, and the staff started speaking quietly so they wouldn't wake her. When she woke up, they even played with her!’ There's also a pretty spacious kids' play area with lots of toys, including a mini kitchen.

Charlottenburg, Berlin
BRAND
NEW
Manifesto

Manifesto is a food court inside The Playce at Potsdamer Platz, with 14 restaurant options available. They recently reopened the play area (October 2026), located beneath the venue’s staircases – which serve as bleachers for those who want to eat, drink, or watch the big screen on site. A follower who lives nearby and is a regular, however, warns: “It gets really crowded really fast, and the age range for the toys is pretty narrow.” There is a changing table on site.

Mitte, Berlin

Free indoor spots for kids

Rainy or cold? Free places for babies and toddlers.

HIDDEN
GEM
Humboldt Forum

Most people have no idea that the first floor of the Humboldt Forum hides workshop rooms filled with toys, books, games, and other discoveries for the little ones. The venue is open every day except Tuesdays. Even when there are no workshops, the hall remains open. It’s free and open to the public, this means, no pre-registration is required.

Mitte, Berlin
CREATIVE
FUN
Spore

The room called “Baustelle” allows children to express their creativity in its most basic form. There, materials that wouldn’t normally be classified as toys are used for playing, creating, and building. There are pipes, tennis balls, colorful ribbons, cellophane, wooden carts, baskets, and much more. It’s definitely worth a visit.

Neukölln, Berlin
GREAT
TOYS
Ludothek Global

The coolest toys in the world, all gathered in one room. Maybe that’s the simplest way to describe what Ludothek is. Another way to put it: it’s like a library, but for toys. They have everything from classics to the latest releases. And the best part: kids can sign up and borrow toys, games, and stuffed animals. All for free.

Neukölln, Berlin

FAQ: Family-friendly places in Berlin

Where can I find cafés with play areas in Berlin?
Look for Kindercafés or cafés with a Spielecke (kids' play corner). Kiezkind Berlin, Kap Frida and Le Brot are some great options to start with.

Are there family-friendly restaurants in Berlin?
Yes. Some restaurants offer high chairs, children's menus, room for strollers and changing tables. Check facilities at the specific location before visiting.

Where can I go with a toddler in Berlin on a rainy day?
Libraries, cultural spaces and family centres can offer indoor activities for young children. Check opening hours, admission conditions and age suitability before visiting.
