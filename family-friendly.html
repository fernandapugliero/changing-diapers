A sign families can trust

The Fix My Diaper Approved badge recognises places that make a practical effort to welcome families — not just places that call themselves family-friendly.

Approved venues can display the badge so parents know that bringing a baby, toddler or stroller has already been considered.

It is a small signal with a simple message: families are welcome here.

What makes a place family-friendly?

We look at five practical things. None of them need to be fancy. What matters is whether they make an outing easier for a family.

1. Family-friendly vibe Families feel welcome from the moment they arrive. Staff are friendly, patient and practical — whether that means helping with a stroller, finding a comfortable place to sit with a baby or simply treating children as part of the experience rather than a problem.
2. Changing option A changing table, dedicated changing area or another clean and safe surface where a baby can be changed comfortably. Bonus points for diapers, wet wipes or other useful changing amenities.
3. High chair or similar Babies and toddlers may need to eat during the visit. A high chair or another secure seating option gives them a safe place to sit and makes meals much easier for everyone.
4. Stroller-friendly A stroller should not become a problem at the door. Ideally it can enter the venue. If that is not possible, there should be a safe place to leave it while keeping it accessible whenever the family needs it.
5. Something to do Toys, books, building blocks, drawing materials or a simple game can keep children engaged for a little while — giving parents a chance to eat, talk, relax and enjoy being out too.
