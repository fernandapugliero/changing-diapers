Behind the newsletter

Most of it starts outside, not behind a screen.

Having a baby didn't make me want to stop going out. So my son and I keep exploring Berlin: especially its cafés and restaurants, but also museums, classes, playgrounds and services. We try things, notice what works with a little one (and what really doesn't), and share what we find.

It's not just a list of places. I also write about local trends, how families experience the city, policies that affect parents, and our trips beyond Berlin. There are tips and discoveries from other local parents, too.

Because having a child shouldn't mean giving up the city — or the things you used to love doing in it.
