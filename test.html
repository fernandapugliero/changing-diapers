FAQ: Family-Friendly Places in Berlin

Where can I find cafés with play areas in Berlin?
Look for Kindercafés or cafés with a Spielecke (kids' play corner). Some have dedicated indoor play areas, while others offer smaller spaces for children.

Are there family-friendly restaurants in Berlin?
Yes. Many restaurants welcome families, but facilities vary. Play areas, high chairs, stroller access and changing tables are worth checking before visiting.

Where can I go with a toddler in Berlin on a rainy day?
Libraries, family centres and some community spaces offer indoor activities for young children. Check opening hours, age limits and whether registration is required.
