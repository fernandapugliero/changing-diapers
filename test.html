What Makes a Café or Restaurant Family-Friendly?

Not every place that welcomes children is equally practical for parents. Here are three things worth checking before heading out.

Cafés with Play Areas

Indoor play corners and dedicated kids' spaces

Look for a play area suited to your child's age. Some cafés have small corners, while others offer more space to play.

FAQ: Family-Friendly Cafés & Restaurants in Berlin

Where can I find cafés with kids' play areas in Berlin?
Look for Kindercafés or cafés with a Spielecke (play corner). These can be found in several Berlin neighbourhoods, including Prenzlauer Berg, Friedrichshain and Kreuzberg.

Are family-friendly restaurants in Berlin also baby-friendly?
Not always. Some welcome children but don't have changing tables, stroller access or high chairs. It's worth checking before visiting.
