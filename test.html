FAQ

Where can I find cafés with play areas in Berlin?
Look for Kindercafés or cafés with a Spielecke (kids' play corner). Play areas and age suitability vary by venue.

Are there free indoor activities for toddlers in Berlin?
Yes. Some family centres, libraries and community spaces offer free indoor activities. Check age requirements, opening hours and registration rules before visiting.
